# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in `schematic-mcp` a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or role.

## Expected behavior

Examples of positive behavior include:

- being respectful and constructive in technical discussion;
- focusing criticism on code, design, evidence, and outcomes rather than people;
- welcoming newcomers and explaining project-specific context;
- respecting differing engineering constraints and EDA workflows;
- reporting security-sensitive problems privately.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, publishing private information without permission, or other conduct that makes collaboration unsafe.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, community spaces, and when representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and comments that violate this policy, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

Concerns should be reported privately to the maintainer through the contact method listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy is intentionally concise for the project's current size and may be replaced by a more formal community code of conduct as the contributor community grows.
